**Contract (CLOUD's).** `docs/runners-contract.md` in the cortexkit-cloud repository is canonical, with vectors in its `test-vectors/runners/` (agreed by CLOUD and MOTOR on 2026-10-17; design section 5). Motor mirrors both through a digest manifest: the contract text and vectors are copied into `motor-lease` with their SHA-256s, and a test checks them, as cortexkit-cloud pins callosum's contract. This campaign authors no contract and no second vector set. Until CLOUD gives the commit, the fake broker and tests use vectors generated from the exact formats below; before slice 6 merges they are replaced by CLOUD's pinned vectors through the manifest. Values marked *interim* are this spec's reading where the agreed formats are silent; the mirrored contract text replaces any that differ before slice 6 merges.
- Job token: compact JWS with header `{alg:"EdDSA", kid, typ:"ck-job+jwt"}`. The host refuses any other `alg` or `typ`, an unknown or removed `kid`, and any `crit`. Claims:
  - `iss` = `https://runners.cortexkit.io`. The key set is at `https://runners.cortexkit.io/.well-known/jwks.json` (Ed25519 OKP keys). A next key is announced at least 7 days before it signs. The host pins the issuer and caches the set. *Interim:* the host agent (not `motor-lease`, which does no I/O) fetches the set at start and every 10 min, keeps the last good set when a fetch fails or returns no keys, and refuses every token with `auth` until a first set is fetched.
  - `aud` = the host id; `jti` = the job id; `sub` = `acct:<ULID>` or `org:<ULID>`; `purpose` = `run` | `attach`.
  - `iat` and `exp` (unix seconds; `exp` about 5 minutes after `iat`, checked at connect only; clock skew 60 s).
  - `vcpus` and `mem_mib` (integers; `mem_mib` must equal `memory_max(vcpus)` in MiB), `platform` (string), `net` (boolean: `false` is the runner's `none`, `true` is `outbound`), `placement` = `{host, vm}`, `max_s`.
  - `cnf` = `{jkt}`: base64url, no padding, of the SHA-256 RFC 7638 JWK thumbprint of the requester's OKP public key.
- Lease routes. Both carry `Authorization: Bearer <machine worker token>`, audience `cortexkit-runners`.
  - `POST https://runners.cortexkit.io/v1/leases`, body `{job_id, vcpus, mem_mib, platform, net, workspace_key_hash, repo_hash, placement_hint|null, timeout_s, requester_jwk}`. Reply 201 `{lease_id, job_id, placement:{host,vm}, endpoint, endpoint_spki_sha256, job_token, token_expires_at_ms, max_s, reserved_vcpu_ms}`. `reserved_vcpu_ms` = `vcpus × max(timeout_s, 10) × 1000`.
  - *Interim body values:* `workspace_key_hash` and `repo_hash` are base64url (no padding) SHA-256 of the UTF-8 workspace key and repository identity ck-motor already sends on Run, so a re-request for the same workspace is not a field difference; `placement_hint` is the stored `{host, vm}` of the workspace's last lease, else null. `endpoint` is `<host>:<port>`, dialled with TLS.
  - `POST /v1/leases/{job_id}/reattach`, body `{requester_jwk}`. Reply 200 with the same fields and an `attach` token, or 409 `job_finished` once the job is settled.
  - `requester_jwk` is exactly `{kty:"OKP", crv:"Ed25519", x}`.
  - The broker keeps one reservation per `job_id`. While a heartbeat reports the job `reserved`, a lease re-request returns a fresh `run` token on the same reservation, bound to the new `requester_jwk`; any other field differing gets 409 `job_conflict`. After a heartbeat reports `admitted` or `started`, a re-request gets 409 `job_admitted`, and recovery is reattach. *Interim:* before any heartbeat lists the job, a re-request is treated as `reserved`; once settled it gets 409 `job_finished`.
  - There is no release route. A reservation whose job never reaches a host (a final `host_full`, `capacity`, `auth` or `expired`, a connect failure, or `max_s < 10` refused by ck-motor) is held until the broker's lost-host settlement, which settles it at 0 because no heartbeat ever reported elapsed time for it.
  - `endpoint_spki_sha256` is base64url (no padding) of the SHA-256 of the host certificate's DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo. The operator's host-registration script registers it together with the host's Ed25519 key and endpoint. ck-motor pins it and does no name or WebPKI checks, so a self-signed certificate with that SPKI is valid. A mismatch is refused before the token is sent.
- Prelude frames, length-prefixed JSON with a `type` field (docs/wire-protocol.md:26-28): `lease_hello {v: 1, token, requester_jwk}`; `challenge {nonce: 64 lowercase hex chars, host_id, ts: unix seconds}`; `challenge_response {sig: base64url unpadded Ed25519}`; `lease_ok {}`; `lease_refused {reason}`. Unknown fields are ignored; an unknown `type` or a `v` other than 1 closes the connection.
- If the `lease_hello` JWK's RFC 7638 thumbprint is not `cnf.jkt`, the host refuses with `auth` before the challenge. The challenge signature is checked against that key only.
- Requester challenge: the signed bytes are UTF-8 `motor-host-challenge-v1\n<nonce hex, 32 bytes>\n<placement.host>\n<jti>\n<unix seconds>\n`. The challenge frame's `host_id` must equal the token's `placement.host`. The client signs the `placement.host` from its lease and refuses a challenge whose `host_id` differs. The host refuses a reused nonce and skew over 60 s, and allows at most one live connection per (`jti`, `purpose`); a pre-admit run token replaces the live run connection as pinned under Connection and admission.
- Tenant binding: provisioning writes `/etc/motor-host/tenants.json` (root-owned, readable by `motor-host`), mapping each `placement.vm` to `{tenant, sub}`. Before the challenge the host requires `aud` and `placement.host` to equal its host id, `placement.vm` to be in the map, and `sub` to equal the entry's `sub`; otherwise `lease_refused {auth}`, with no wake, disk attach, directory creation or usage. The journal records each `jti`'s tenant and `sub`, and a later token for that `jti` must match both.
- Host messages: every host request carries `CK-Host: <host_id>`, `CK-Timestamp: <unix ms>`, `CK-Counter: <positive integer>` and `CK-Signature: <base64url Ed25519>`. The signature is over the UTF-8 of `ck-host-v1\n<METHOD>\n<path>\n<sha256 hex of body>\n<timestamp>\n<counter>`. The broker answers 401 `{"error":"host_auth_failed"}` to a counter not above the last accepted one, a timestamp more than 60 s off, or an unregistered host.
  - The host fsyncs its counter to its journal before signing, and seeds it on start at `max(persisted + 1, unix_ms × 1000)`. A retry re-signs the unchanged body with a fresh counter and timestamp. The host keeps at most one unacknowledged signed request in flight; heartbeats and usage queue behind it in counter order. A 401 after the clock went backwards is an operator alert: retire the host key and register a new one.
- Heartbeat: `POST /v1/hosts/{host_id}/heartbeat` every 15-30 s (default 15 s) and on change. Body `{free_vcpus, free_mem_mib, queued, warm:[{tenant, workspace_key_hash, vm}], running:[{job_id, state, elapsed_ms}], healthy}`. `running` lists each non-ended job: Reserved and Released leases report `reserved`, Admitted `admitted`, Started `started`; `elapsed_ms` is the host-measured command run time. Jobs not placed on the host come back in `{"ignored":[...]}`. *Interim values:* `free_vcpus` is Σ over tenants of `vcpu_ceiling` minus its quota share (Caps); `free_mem_mib` is host `MemAvailable` in MiB; `queued` counts Reserved leases not yet delivered; `warm` lists running and suspended VMs; `healthy` is journal writable and helper reachable.
- Usage: `POST /v1/hosts/{host_id}/usage`, once per job when it ends. Body `{job_id, vcpus, started_at_ms, ended_at_ms, mem_peak_mib, egress_bytes, outcome}`, `outcome` one of `exit`, `signal`, `timeout`, `cancelled` or `lost`. The reply is `{job_id, settled_vcpu_ms, released_vcpu_ms}`; settlement is `min(vcpus × (ended − started), reserved_vcpu_ms)`, all in ms. An identical resend returns the same reply. A different body gets 409 `usage_conflict`, a job not placed here 404 `job_unknown`, and a job already settled as `host_lost` 409 `job_settled`.
  - `outcome` mapping: a runner terminal with `killed: deadline`, or any terminal after the host's `max_s` cancel, is `timeout`; `killed: cancel` is `cancelled`; otherwise `exit` and `signal` map to themselves; `cancelled`, `refused_before_start`, a port-53 `refused` and any job that never started are `cancelled`; a watchdog or loss settlement is `lost` (the client still sees `outcome_unknown`).
  - `mem_peak_mib` is the maximum of the VM cgroup's `memory.current`, sampled at each heartbeat and at the end, over the job's Started-to-end window; `egress_bytes` is the delta of the tenant tap's forward-rule byte counter over that window. Both are integers when no other lease was Reserved, Admitted or Started during the window, and JSON null otherwise.
  - Usage reports sit in a durable outbox. An entry leaves it after a 2xx, or after one of those three replies, which are final: logged for operator reconciliation, never retried, and the queue advances. Any other failure is retried with a fresh signature. An entry still unacknowledged after 30 days moves to `/var/lib/motor-host/outbox-expired/`, logs `usage_outbox_expired`, and the queue advances.
- Lost host: with no usage report by `iat + max_s + 10 min`, the broker settles the job at `vcpus × last heartbeat elapsed_ms` with reason `host_lost`.
